# Mathematics: Linear Functions

**Grade:** 8

**Learning objective:** Identify slope and y-intercept in y = mx + b, explain their meanings, convert simple relationships between equations and tables, and solve basic real-world linear relationship problems.

## Explanation

A linear function describes a relationship with a constant rate of change between two quantities: the input, represented by x, and the output, represented by y. The most common way to write a linear function is in slope-intercept form: y = mx + b. In this equation, m stands for the slope, which is the constant rate of change. It tells you how much y increases or decreases every time x increases by 1. The letter b stands for the y-intercept, which represents the initial value, or starting value. On a graph, the y-intercept is the point where the line crosses the vertical y-axis, corresponding to the coordinate pair (0, b). In real-world contexts, the y-intercept is often a one-time fixed fee, a starting balance, or an initial amount, while the slope is a recurring rate, such as a cost per hour, a speed in miles per hour, or a change in temperature per minute. To make an input-output table from an equation, choose or use given values for x, substitute each into the equation for x, and calculate the corresponding value of y. To write an equation from a table, first find the slope by determining the change in y divided by the change in x between consecutive rows. Then, find the value of y when x is 0 to get the y-intercept. Putting these two values together gives you the equation y = mx + b, which can be used to predict outputs or solve practical problems.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Identifying Slope and Y-Intercept in an Equation

Identify the slope and the y-intercept of the linear equation y = -3x + 8.

1. Compare the given equation y = -3x + 8 to the standard slope-intercept form y = mx + b.
2. Locate the number multiplied by x, which represents the slope m. Here, the coefficient of x is -3.
3. Locate the constant term added at the end, which represents the y-intercept b. Here, the constant term is 8.

**Answer:** The slope m is -3, and the y-intercept b is 8.

### Example 2: Creating a Table from a Linear Equation

Use the equation y = 4x - 5 to find the missing output values for x = 0, 1, 2, and 3.

1. Substitute x = 0 into the equation: y = 4(0) - 5 = 0 - 5 = -5.
2. Substitute x = 1 into the equation: y = 4(1) - 5 = 4 - 5 = -1.
3. Substitute x = 2 into the equation: y = 4(2) - 5 = 8 - 5 = 3.
4. Substitute x = 3 into the equation: y = 4(3) - 5 = 12 - 5 = 7.

**Answer:** When x is 0, y is -5; when x is 1, y is -1; when x is 2, y is 3; and when x is 3, y is 7.

### Example 3: Writing an Equation from a Table and Modeling Real-World Context

A car rental company charges according to the table: at 0 miles, cost is $25; at 10 miles, cost is $27; at 20 miles, cost is $29; at 30 miles, cost is $31. Let x be the miles driven and y be the total cost in dollars. Write an equation in y = mx + b form and find the total cost to drive 75 miles.

1. Find the rate of change (slope m): as x increases by 10 miles (from 0 to 10), y increases by $2 (from 25 to 27). The slope is change in y divided by change in x, which is 2 / 10 = 0.20.
2. Find the initial value (y-intercept b): when x = 0 miles, y = 25. Therefore, b = 25.
3. Write the equation in y = mx + b form using m = 0.20 and b = 25: y = 0.2x + 25.
4. Calculate the cost for 75 miles by substituting x = 75: y = 0.2(75) + 25 = 15 + 25 = 40.

**Answer:** The equation is y = 0.2x + 25, and the total cost for driving 75 miles is $40.

## Summary

- The slope-intercept form of a linear equation is y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.
- The slope (m) represents the constant rate of change, showing how much y changes per one unit increase in x.
- The y-intercept (b) represents the initial amount or starting value, which is the value of y when x = 0.
- You can convert an equation into a table by substituting x-values to compute y-values.
- You can convert a table into an equation by finding the constant rate of change (change in y divided by change in x) and reading the y-value where x = 0.

## Exercises

1. Identify the slope (m) and y-intercept (b) for each linear equation: (a) y = 7x - 4 and (b) y = -x + 12.
2. A plumber charges a total fee modeled by the equation y = 45x + 60, where x is the number of hours worked and y is the total cost in dollars. Explain the practical meaning of the slope 45 and the y-intercept 60 in this scenario.
3. Use the equation y = -2x + 9 to find the missing values of y for x = 0, x = 1, x = 2, and x = 5.
4. A table shows the following values: (x = 0, y = 14), (x = 2, y = 20), (x = 4, y = 26), and (x = 6, y = 32). Determine the rate of change and the initial value, then write the linear equation in y = mx + b form.
5. Maya opens a savings account with an initial deposit of $150 and plans to save $35 each week. Write a linear equation relating the total savings (y) to the number of weeks (x). Then, calculate how much money Maya will have saved after 12 weeks.

## Solutions

1. For part (a), compare y = 7x - 4 to y = mx + b to find slope m = 7 and y-intercept b = -4. For part (b), the term -x is equivalent to -1x, so the slope m = -1 and the y-intercept b = 12.
2. The slope of 45 represents the hourly labor rate, meaning the plumber charges $45 for each additional hour worked. The y-intercept of 60 represents the fixed initial fee or service call charge, meaning the customer pays $60 just for the visit before any hours of work begin.
3. For x = 0: y = -2(0) + 9 = 9. For x = 1: y = -2(1) + 9 = -2 + 9 = 7. For x = 2: y = -2(2) + 9 = -4 + 9 = 5. For x = 5: y = -2(5) + 9 = -10 + 9 = -1.
4. The change in y between consecutive entries is 20 - 14 = 6, and the change in x is 2 - 0 = 2. The slope m is change in y divided by change in x, which is 6 / 2 = 3. The initial value b is the value of y when x = 0, which is 14. Combining these gives the equation y = 3x + 14.
5. The initial amount is the y-intercept, b = 150. The weekly rate of savings is the slope, m = 35. The equation is y = 35x + 150. To find the amount saved after 12 weeks, substitute x = 12: y = 35(12) + 150 = 420 + 150 = 570. Maya will have saved $570 after 12 weeks.

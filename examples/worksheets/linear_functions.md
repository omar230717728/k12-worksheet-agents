# Mathematics: Linear Functions

**Grade:** 8

**Learning objective:** Identify slope and y-intercept in y = mx + b, explain their meanings, convert simple relationships between equations and tables, and solve basic real-world linear relationship problems.

## Explanation

A linear function is a relationship between two variables that can be written in the slope-intercept form y = mx + b. In this equation, x represents the independent variable and y represents the dependent variable. The letter m represents the slope, which is the rate of change or how much y increases or decreases for every step increase of 1 in x. The letter b represents the y-intercept, which is the initial value or the starting amount when x is equal to 0. By understanding these two components, you can easily graph linear functions, interpret tables of values, and solve real-world problems involving constant rates.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Identifying Slope and Y-Intercept from an Equation

Identify the slope and y-intercept for the linear equation y = 3x + 5.

1. Compare the given equation to the standard slope-intercept form y = mx + b.
2. Locate the coefficient of x to find the slope m.
3. Locate the constant term to find the y-intercept b.

**Answer:** The slope m is 3, and the y-intercept b is 5.

### Example 2: Writing an Equation from a Table

Find the slope and y-intercept from the table where x values are 0, 1, 2 and y values are 4, 7, 10, then write the equation.

1. Find the y-intercept b by looking for the y-value when x is 0. Here, y is 4 when x is 0, so b = 4.
2. Find the slope m by calculating the change in y divided by the change in x between any two rows. As x increases by 1 from 0 to 1, y increases by 3 from 4 to 7. So m = 3.
3. Substitute m and b into the slope-intercept form y = mx + b.

**Answer:** y = 3x + 4

### Example 3: Solving a Real-World Linear Problem

A phone repair shop charges a fixed fee of 50 dollars plus 20 dollars per hour of labor. Write an equation to represent the total cost y for x hours of labor, and find the cost for 3 hours of work.

1. Identify the initial value (fixed fee) as the y-intercept b = 50.
2. Identify the rate of change (cost per hour) as the slope m = 20.
3. Write the equation in the form y = 20x + 50.
4. Substitute 3 for x to find the cost for 3 hours: y = 20(3) + 50 = 60 + 50 = 110.

**Answer:** The equation is y = 20x + 50, and the total cost for 3 hours of work is 110 dollars.

## Summary

- The slope-intercept form of a linear equation is written as y = mx + b.
- The slope m represents the rate of change or steepness of the line.
- The y-intercept b represents the initial value or starting amount when x is zero.
- Tables can be used to find both the slope and y-intercept by observing the patterns of change between input and output values.
- Real-world situations can be modeled using linear equations by identifying fixed starting costs and variable rates.

## Exercises

1. Identify the slope and y-intercept in the equation y = 4x - 7.
2. Identify the slope and y-intercept in the equation y = -2x + 9.
3. A gym membership costs 30 dollars to join plus 15 dollars per month. Write an equation to represent the total cost y after x months.
4. Given the table where x is 0, 1, 2 and y is 5, 8, 11, find the slope, y-intercept, and write the equation.
5. A candle starts at a height of 12 inches and burns down by 2 inches every hour. Write the equation for the height y after x hours.

## Solutions

1. Slope m = 4, y-intercept b = -7
2. Slope m = -2, y-intercept b = 9
3. y = 15x + 30
4. Slope m = 3, y-intercept b = 5, Equation: y = 3x + 5
5. y = -2x + 12
